Company snapshot

Who we are

AC Mechanical Refrigeration Ltd. is an Ottawa-based mechanical, HVAC-R and refrigeration contractor established in 1972. For more than 50 years we have served commercial and industrial clients across Ottawa and eastern Ontario, and we currently service 40+ facilities on an ongoing basis with an experienced, long-tenured team.

Through our affiliation with Optimum Mechanical, we self-perform the full mechanical scope — plumbing, sheet metal, welding, electrical and elevator — so public-sector buyers can procure the entire mechanical package from one accountable contractor.
