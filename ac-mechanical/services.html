Refrigeration

Industrial Refrigeration & Ice-Plant Systems

Design support, installation, service and modernization of industrial refrigeration and ice-plant systems — the specialized, high-consequence equipment that keeps arenas, processing facilities and cold-storage operations running.

Typical applications

  • Ice-plant / ice-rink refrigeration systems
  • Cold storage and process refrigeration
  • Compressor, condenser and evaporator service and replacement
  • Controls, safety systems and refrigerant management

Sectors served

Commercial · Industrial · Institutional (arenas, recreation complexes, food & processing facilities) · target growth in government and municipal recreation assets.

HVAC-R

Commercial & Industrial HVAC-R

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration for demanding commercial and industrial buildings — engineered for reliability, energy performance and indoor-environment requirements.

Typical applications

  • Building HVAC systems: air conditioning, ventilation and air distribution
  • Integrated heating and refrigeration for mixed-use and industrial facilities
  • System upgrades, retrofits and energy-efficiency improvements
  • Controls integration and performance troubleshooting

Sectors served

Office and institutional buildings · industrial plants · recreation and healthcare facilities · federal and municipal properties (target growth).

Discuss an HVAC-R project

Process Cooling

Chillers & Process Cooling — Ammonia, Natural Gas & Propane

Chiller and process-cooling systems across refrigerant and fuel types, including ammonia, natural gas and propane systems. Specialized handling for the code, safety and operator-training considerations these systems demand.

Typical applications

  • Ammonia (NH₃) refrigeration and process chilling
  • Natural-gas and propane-fired systems
  • Chiller installation, overhaul and preventive maintenance
  • Safety systems, monitoring and regulatory compliance support

Sectors served

Industrial process facilities · arenas and recreation · food and beverage · institutional central plants.

Request a chiller assessment

Heating

Commercial & Industrial Heating

Complete heating capability for large buildings and plants — boilers, hydronic systems and the air-side equipment that distributes and conditions heat throughout a facility.

Systems we install & service

  • Boilers and boiler-room systems
  • Hydronic (hot-water) heating systems
  • Rooftop units (RTUs)
  • Air handlers
  • Make-up air units

Sectors served

Institutional and government buildings · industrial plants · multi-building campuses and central heating plants.
