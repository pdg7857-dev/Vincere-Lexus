Fifty years of mechanical expertise, rooted in eastern Ontario

AC Mechanical Refrigeration Ltd. has served Ottawa and eastern Ontario since 1972 — building the certifications, clearance and reputation that public-sector and industrial clients rely on.

Our history

Established in Ottawa, 1972

For more than five decades, AC Mechanical Refrigeration Ltd. has designed, installed, serviced and maintained mechanical, HVAC-R and refrigeration systems across the National Capital Region and eastern Ontario. What began in 1972 has grown into a trusted contractor servicing 40+ facilities on an ongoing basis.

Our longevity is itself a qualification: fifty-plus years of continuous operation, a stable and experienced team, and the accumulated know-how to keep complex mechanical systems running in demanding commercial, industrial and institutional environments.

Today we are focused on bringing that same reliability to government and institutional clients — federal, provincial and municipal facilities, universities, colleges, hospitals, school boards and transit — where our security clearance, certifications and single-source capability are a strong fit.

Single-source mechanical

The Optimum Mechanical affiliation

Through our affiliation with Optimum Mechanical, AC Mechanical self-performs the full mechanical scope — extending beyond HVAC-R and refrigeration to include plumbing, sheet-metal fabrication and installation, welding, electrical and elevator services.

What single-source means for you: one contract, one accountable team and one coordinated schedule for the entire mechanical package — fewer interfaces, less coordination risk, and consistent quality and safety standards across every trade. For public-sector buyers, that translates to simpler procurement and clearer accountability.

Safety-first culture

Safety is how we protect your facility

Working on live mechanical, refrigeration and pressure systems — often inside occupied or secure facilities — demands discipline. Safety is embedded in how we plan, staff and execute every job, protecting our people, your building and your project schedule.

  • Documented health & safety practices and site-specific safety plans
  • WSIB coverage in good standing
  • Trained, ticketed tradespeople for regulated systems (e.g. ammonia, gas)
  • Security-cleared personnel for controlled environments
