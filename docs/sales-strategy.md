# Sales & Relationship Playbook

A running list of ideas for upselling and showing customers why they want to
work with me. Add to this freely — capture first, refine later.

## Trust & social proof

- **Cover the desk in reviews.** Print every review/testimonial, laminate them,
  and tape them to my desk — goal is to cover the *entire* surface. When I'm away
  (getting keys, pulling a car around, talking to a manager), customers sitting at
  the desk will read the reviews and arrive at the close already trusting me.
  - _Next steps:_ collect every review I have (Google, dealer site, texts,
    emails), pick the strongest quotes, print + laminate, refresh as new ones come in.

## Cross-sell from other channels

- **Tap my gov-contract software clients.** Every client I work with through my
  government-contract software is a future Lexus prospect. Stay in touch, build
  the relationship there, and bring them over to Lexus when the timing is right.
  - _Next steps:_ keep a list of those contacts and where each one is in the
    relationship so I can reach out when there's a natural opening.

## Volume & wholesale channels

- **Build relationships with used-car exporters.** Exporters who buy used cars
  are a path to volume sales — one relationship can move many units at once.
  - _Next steps:_ identify the active exporters in my market, get introductions,
    and understand what specs/price points they want so I can feed them deals.

## My own CRM / stock-match alerts

- **Track every single interaction in my own CRM.** Log what each customer is
  looking for (model, trim, colour, budget, timing). When a matching car comes
  into stock, the system notifies me so I can text/call that customer right away
  — first to reach them, first to close.
  - _Next steps:_ decide where this lives (this app already has inventory data +
    a wants-matcher, so an alert layer that watches `data/inventory.json` against
    saved customer wants is a natural fit), and start logging every interaction now.
