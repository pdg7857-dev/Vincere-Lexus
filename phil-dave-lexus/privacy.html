Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Your details stay with me. They are never sold, and I only collect what I need to find you the right car and stay in touch.

This policy explains what information I collect through this website, why, and your choices about it. I am Phil Dave, a registered automotive salesperson sourcing vehicles for clients in the Greater Toronto Area, working through a licensed Ontario motor vehicle dealership. You can reach me any time at pgelinas@northwestlexus.com or 514-943-3730.

What I collect

When you contact me through a form (Request a Car, Find your Lexus, the Lexus reference tool, or an inquiry from The Lot), I collect the details you choose to give me — your name, phone number, email, the vehicle you want, and any preferences such as make, model, trim, colour, budget and timeline, plus any notes you add.

How you use The Lot

The Lot (my used-inventory listings) is open to browse. To understand what visitors are looking for and improve what I show, I keep a simple record of how The Lot is used: the searches and filters you run, the vehicles you view or inquire about, the price points you focus on, timestamps, and how long a visit lasts.

Browsing is recorded against an anonymous visitor ID, not your name. If you have not given me your contact details, this activity is not linked to any personal information — it is stored under a random ID generated by your browser. As soon as you contact me through a form, the details you provide are, of course, linked to your enquiry.

Why I collect it

  • To find, vet and present vehicles that match what you want.
  • To contact you about your enquiry and about vehicles that may suit you.
  • To understand your preferences so I waste less of your time.
  • To complete a purchase through the licensed dealership when you are ready.

Contact consent

When you submit a form you agree that I may contact you by phone, text and email about your enquiry, in line with Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL). You can withdraw that consent and ask me to stop contacting you at any time — just reply to any message or email me directly.

How it is stored and who sees it

Your information is stored in a private spreadsheet hosted on Google Workspace, accessible only to me. I do not sell, rent or trade your information. I may share the details necessary to complete a vehicle purchase with the licensed dealership I work through, and only for that purpose.

Vehicle photos shown in The Lot are served from the dealership's inventory provider. This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me at any time to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do so, email pgelinas@northwestlexus.com and I will take care of it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle information — for example, if I add website analytics — I will update this page and the date above.
